Say it naturally

Hoi! Hoe gaat het?Hi! How are you?
Dank je wel. · Alsjeblieft.Thank you. · Please / Here you go.
Geen probleem. Tot straks!No problem. See you later!
Gezellig!Pleasantly social, cosy or enjoyable; exact meaning depends on context.
Doei! / Tot ziens!Bye! / Goodbye!
